About Me

Hi! I am Meng Gao, a master's student at Tsinghua University and a member of IIGroup, supervised by Prof. Yujiu Yang. Previously, I received my Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from Fudan University's Talent Program.

My research interests include reinforcement learning, efficient inference, and multimodal interaction. I am also interested in the mathematical principles behind learning algorithms.

I am currently a research intern at the Qwen-Omni Team, Alibaba. Previously, I interned at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA).

I expect to graduate in 2027 and plan to apply for PhD programs. Feel free to contact me if you are interested in my work or potential collaboration.
